# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want participation in `QuantumEntanglementTools` to be safe, respectful, and
constructive. Be precise about technical disagreements, assume good faith,
credit others' work, welcome questions, and make space for people with different
backgrounds and levels of experience.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, sustained disruption, plagiarism,
license evasion, and retaliation against someone who reports a concern.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository spaces, project communication channels,
project events, and public interactions where someone is representing the
project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `jmartin@uliege.be`. Include relevant links,
dates, context, and any immediate safety concern. Do not publish sensitive
personal information in an issue.

Maintainers may remove content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict
participation, or permanently exclude a participant, proportionate to the
behavior and risk. Reports will be shared only as needed to assess and address
the concern. Anyone with a conflict of interest must recuse themselves from the
decision. No policy can guarantee confidentiality where law or immediate safety
requires disclosure, but privacy will be protected as far as practical.

Good-faith reporters and participants in an investigation must not be
retaliated against.
